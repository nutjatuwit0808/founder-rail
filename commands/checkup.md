description: Health report for the whole project in plain language — run monthly or before big decisions
---

Run a project health check:

1. Invoke the `founder-rail:health-check` skill and complete it fully.
2. Read-only: nothing is fixed during a checkup. Every issue found comes back as a plain-language item with an offer — actual fixing goes through `/founder-rail:fix` (broken things) or `/founder-rail:idea` (improvements).
